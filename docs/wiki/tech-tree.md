## 📖 How the tree works

| | |
|---|---|
| 🏛️ **Built from the 100 ages** | Each discovery belongs to one of the [Ages of Civilization](ages-of-civilization.md "Real-history ages from stone tools to intergalactic"). |
| 🌿 **100 branches** | Ten families of ten branches, growing side by side. |
| 🤏 **They pick, you push** | Civilizations research on their own. Powers like **Inspiration** (67) and **Dark Age** (77) speed them up or hold them back. |
| 🔗 **Branches feed each other** | Metal feeds war, war feeds rockets, rockets feed space. |

> 🔗 A discovery needs the one before it **on its own branch**, plus any links shown with ➕.

## 🌳 How the families grow

The trees below show how each family grows through the ages.

### 🪓 Tools and Building tree

```
Chopping stones (1) ─ Shaped tools (2) ─ Shelters (7) ─ Villages (16)
   └─ Cities (23) ─ Monuments (31) ─ Engineering (38) ─ Castles (44)
      └─ Machine tools (64) ─ Mass production (75) ─ Robots (88)
         └─ Space stations (91) ─ Terraforming (94) ─ Dyson sphere (98)
```

### 🔥 Energy tree

```
Fire (3) ─ Hearth (4) ─ Mills (45) ─ Steam (63) ─ Electricity (68)
   └─ Oil (70) ─ Engines (72) ─ The Atom (78) ─ Clean power (86)
      └─ Fusion (89) ─ The Swarm (97) ─ The Dyson Sphere (98)
```

### ⚒️ Materials tree

```
Stone (1) ─ Pottery (17) ─ Copper (21) ─ Bronze (25) ─ Iron (32)
   └─ Steel (66) ─ Electronics (79) ─ Tiny machines (90)
      └─ Asteroid mining (95) ➕ needs Planet hoppers (93)
```

### 💊 Medicine tree

```
Herbs (40) ─ Plague quarantine (49) ─ Vaccines (62) ─ Germ theory (69)
   └─ Antibiotics (77) ─ Reading DNA (84) ─ Writing DNA (85)
      ➕ needs Science (57)
```

### ⚔️ War tree

```
Spears (8) ─ Bow (10) ─ Bronze weapons (25) ─ Iron weapons (32)
   └─ Standing armies (39) ─ Castles (44) ─ Gunpowder (48)
      └─ World wars (76) ─ The bomb (78) ─ Rockets (80)
         └─ Star travelers (96) ─ Intergalactic war (100)
```

> ⚠️ **The danger branch.** Every step up here makes self-destruction easier.

### 🚀 Travel and Space tree

```
Walking ─ Sails (29) ─ Roads (37) ─ Compass (47) ─ Exploration (54)
   └─ Railways (65) ─ Engines (72) ─ Flight (73) ─ Rockets (80)
      └─ Satellites (81) ─ The Moon (82) ─ Moon bases (92)
         └─ Planet hoppers (93) ─ Star travelers (96)
            └─ Galactic (99) ─ Intergalactic (100)
```

### 🗣️ Culture and Politics tree

```
Speech (5) ─ Spirit (11) ─ Bands (12) ─ Chiefdoms (20) ─ Kingdoms (26)
   └─ Law (30) ─ Philosophy (35) ─ Empires (36) ─ Lords and lands (43)
      └─ Enlightenment (60) ─ Revolutions (61) ─ The Network (83)
         └─ Galactic empire (99)
```

### 🧬 Life and Harvest — how your people harvest life for you

> *At first, you gather [Souls](glossary.md#souls "The energy you spend on god powers") yourself. It's slow, and if they see you, it scares them. Then they learn to do it for you.*

This is decided: later in the game, intelligent life uses its own powers to harvest life for you. In the endgame, harvesting life for its own needs becomes intelligent life's goal. Like a virus, it spreads, uses up nutrients and resources, and has to keep spreading.

```
Tamed wolves (13) ─ Farming (14) ─ Herding (15) ─ Irrigation (19)
   └─ Spirit / sacrifice (11) ➕ ─ Kingdoms (26)
      └─ Factories (64) ─ Mass production (75)
         └─ Writing DNA (85) ─ Thinking machines (87)
            └─ Terraforming (94) ─ Galactic (99) ─ Intergalactic (100)
```
