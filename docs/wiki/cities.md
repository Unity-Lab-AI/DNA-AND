## 🏙️ Settlements, layouts and infrastructure

### Settlement sizes

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Camp** | A temporary group of shelters |
| **Hamlet** | A handful of homes |
| **Village** | Homes and farms around a center |
| **Town** | A market, workshops and a few hundred to thousands of people |
| **City** | A dense center of trade, rule and belief |
| **Metropolis** | A huge city that leads a whole region |
| **Megalopolis** | Cities grown together into one endless urban chain |
| **Planet-city** *(predicted by science)* | A whole world covered by one city |
| **Arcology** *(predicted by science)* | A whole city inside one giant building |

### Layouts

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Organic layout** | No plan; narrow winding streets that follow the land; old cities |
| **Grid layout** | Straight streets crossing at right angles; planned cities |
| **Radial layout** | Streets spreading like spokes from a central square |
| **Linear layout** | A long town along one river, road or coast |
| **Clustered layout** | Tight groups of buildings with open land between |
| **Networked layout** | Many centers joined by transport lines |
| **Walled layout** | A ring of walls with the city packed inside |
| **Concentric rings** | Rings of districts around an old core, newer rings outside |

**How it works in the game:**

- Every settlement, building and road is generated live; nothing is pre-built
- Cities follow the builder species' body plan and genes: insect species build hive cities of segmented cells, burrowers build underground, fliers build tall towers and roosts, swimmers build reef and underwater cities, giants build huge, small species build dense
- A settlement climbs the 100 grouping steps (camp, hamlet, village, town, city…), growing districts and infrastructure as it goes
- The civilization's age decides its materials and infrastructure, from mud and paths to composites and spaceports
- Cities grow along rivers, coasts and trade routes; hills get defenses; layouts bend around the land
- The city's society shapes it: temples tower in theocracies, palaces in monarchies, factories in industrial economies (100 kinds of society)
- On hostile worlds cities need the tech for every world condition: domes, shielded roofs, floating platforms (the 10 × 10 world matrix)
- Collapsed civilizations leave ruins that decay and can be resettled
