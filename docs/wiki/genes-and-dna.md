## 🔤 Real-style DNA

Every living thing in DNA-AND carries a real-style [genome](glossary.md#genome "The full set of DNA instructions for one living thing"), written in the same four letters as real life:

| Letter | Name |
|:-:|---|
| 🟥 **A** | Adenine |
| 🟦 **C** | Cytosine |
| 🟩 **G** | Guanine |
| 🟨 **T** | Thymine |

That DNA builds the body **and** the behavior. Nothing is hand-made: the game's creature generator builds each body **live**, straight from its genes, so every creature is exactly what its genes say it is.

## 🔁 How life changes

| | How | What happens |
|:-:|---|---|
| 🎲 | [Mutation](glossary.md#mutation "A random change in DNA") | Copying errors make random changes. Most are harmless, some kill, a few are brilliant. |
| 💞 | **Breeding** | Two parents mix their genes |
| 🧫 | **Asexual copying** | One parent copies itself, with small errors |
| 🦠 | **Viruses** | Viruses can carry genes from one species to another |

> 🦠 **Even viruses have genomes.** They evolve too — and they can rewrite the creatures they infect.

## 👥 Crowds

You don't need to see every single worm. Creatures of the same kind living in the same area are grouped:

> 🪱 *One worm on screen can stand for 4,396 worms.*

Each group still breeds, mutates, gets sick and evolves. Groups also climb the 100-step [grouping ladder](groups-and-flocking.md "Lone creature to universe-spanning life"), from pack to horde to civilization.

> 😈 **Every gene is selfish.** All life tries to get more and do more before death. And death always comes. Every species and every gene line is tracked in the [Tree of Life](tree-of-life.md "Circular family and gene trees").
