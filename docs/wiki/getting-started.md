## 1️⃣ Make a world

From the main menu, pick **New World**.

| Choice | What it means |
|---|---|
| 🎲 **Seed** | A number that builds your world. Same seed = same galaxy. Leave it blank for a random one. |
| 🌌 **Galaxy size** | How big your playground is. |
| 🌍 **Starting planet** | Earth. Other [templates](glossary.md#template "A planet type with its own rules, unlocked by reaching milestones") unlock as you play. |

🌌 **Galaxy sizes:** Small, Medium, Large or Massive.

## 2️⃣ Meet your dead planet

You start above a young, lifeless world. Hot rock, raw oceans, a thin sky. Nothing is alive — yet.

> ⚠️ **This is not a clicker.** Life won't appear by itself. It starts with **your first action**.

## 4️⃣ Start life

1. 🌊 Find shallow, warm water.
2. ⚗️ Add the building blocks: carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen.
3. ⚡ Strike it with lightning.
4. ✨ Use **Spark of Life**.

If the conditions are right, the first cells appear. If not — try another spot. 🔁

> 💡 **Tip:** Life is picky. Too hot, too cold, too salty, or missing an element, and your spark fizzles.

---

## 5️⃣ Watch, nudge, earn

You never control life directly. You **nudge** it.

- 👀 Watch your cells spread and change.
- 👻 Living things slowly give off [Souls](souls-and-harvesting.md "The energy you spend on god powers").
- 🔓 Spend souls on new [powers](god-powers.md).
- ⏩ Speed time up. The game slows down at each [milestone](glossary.md#milestone "A big step in evolution or history, like first walking or first fire") so you can see it happen.
