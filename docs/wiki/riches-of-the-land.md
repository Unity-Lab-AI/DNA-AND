## ⛏️ Deposits and fields

### Made by magma

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Layered intrusion** | Metals settling in layers inside a slowly cooling body of magma |
| **Kimberlite pipe** | A deep volcanic pipe that carried diamonds up from far below |
| **Pegmatite** | The last, slowest-cooling part of magma, with giant crystals |

### Made by hot fluids

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Porphyry deposit** | A huge, low-grade body of metal veins around a cooled magma body; the world's main copper source |
| **Volcanic massive sulfide** | Metal-rich mounds made by hot vents on the sea floor |
| **Epithermal vein** | Shallow veins left by hot water near volcanoes |
| **Skarn** | Limestone changed by hot fluids from nearby magma |
| **Mountain-belt gold** | Gold veins formed while mountains are built |

### Made by water and time

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Banded iron formation** | Striped layers of iron laid down in ancient seas when oxygen first appeared; needs the oxygen-maker stage of life |
| **Placer** | Heavy grains washed together by rivers and waves |
| **Evaporite** | Minerals left behind when lakes and seas dry up |
| **Sediment-hosted copper** | Copper carried by salty underground water into buried sediments |
| **Coal seam** | Buried ancient forests crushed into black rock; needs forests to have lived |
| **Oil and gas reservoir** | Buried ancient sea life cooked by heat and pressure, trapped under rock; needs life to have lived |

### Made by weather

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Laterite** | Rock rotted by tropical rain and heat, leaving metals behind |
| **Fertile soil** | Broken rock plus the remains of life; grows food |

### Fields in space

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Metal asteroid field** | Iron, nickel, platinum-group metals |
| **Carbon asteroid field** | Water, carbon, life chemistry |
| **Stony asteroid field** | Silicates, metals |
| **Ice field** | Water, methane, ammonia |
| **Regolith** | The dusty broken surface of airless moons and asteroids; helium-3 builds up in it from the star's wind |
| **Giant-planet atmosphere** | Skimmed by fuel scoops |
| **Nebula gas** | Hydrogen, rare gases |
| **Ring ice** | Water ice |
| **Star matter** *(predicted by science)* | Lifted straight off a star by a very advanced civilization |
| **Black-hole energy** *(predicted by science)* | Drawn from a spinning black hole's ergosphere |

**How it works in the game:**

- Deposits follow the planet's geology: magma bodies make magmatic deposits, plate edges and volcanoes make hydrothermal ones, seas and rivers make sedimentary ones, tropical weather makes laterites
- Some resources need life to have existed first: banded iron needs oxygen makers, coal needs forests, oil and gas need ancient sea life
- Planets with plate tectonics make far more ore deposits than stagnant-lid worlds
- Deeper deposits need better mining tech; placers and surface ores come first
- Space fields follow what the bodies are made of (small bodies, giants, nebulas, rings)
- Every deposit and field can run out; civilizations that strip them must move on, as the endgame harvest says
