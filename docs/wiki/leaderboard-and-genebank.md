## 🏆 The Leaderboard

Everyone who plays adds to the leaderboard.

### How your score works

> **Life in your galaxy** ➕ **Life harvested**

| Score part | Grows when… |
|---|---|
| 🌿 **Life in your galaxy** | Your galaxy is full of living things |
| 👻 **Life harvested** | You [harvest](glossary.md#harvest "Reaping living things directly for souls") life, by hand, by disaster, or through your civilizations |

## 🧬 The Genebank

Every player on the leaderboard is listed in the **[Genebank](glossary.md#genebank "The shared library of player genomes")**.

The **top three** can upload a [genome](glossary.md#genome "The full set of DNA instructions for one living thing") from their world for everyone else to use.

🏅 **Genome of the Week:** the top three are locked in once a week. That week's top three get to upload, and their genomes are featured in the Genebank.

---

## ☄️ Bringing a genome into your world

Pick a genome from the Genebank and it arrives in your world by **[meteorite](glossary.md#meteorite "A space rock — and the way Genebank genomes arrive")**, landing in the ocean or on land.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/genebank-meteorite.png | A glowing meteorite streaking down into a shallow sea, a faint helix trail behind it -->
> 🖼️ *A Genebank meteorite falling into a shallow sea* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

| ✅ Plus | ⚠️ Minus |
|---|---|
| New life, already evolved somewhere else | It can take over as an invasive species |

> 💡 **Tip:** Drop a tough genome on a struggling world and watch it take off — or watch it eat everything. 🦖

---

## 🌐 Being online

The leaderboard and Genebank need an internet connection. The rest of the game doesn't.
