## 🚀 Leaving home

When a civilization reaches the space age, everything changes. For the first time, life can spread beyond its home planet.

> ⚠️ **Rule of the galaxy:** life only reaches another planet if **intelligent life gets there first**.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/first-launch.png | A rocket climbing on a pillar of fire from a coastal city at dusk, the planet curving below -->

## 🌱 Spreading life

| Step | What happens |
|:-:|---|
| 1️⃣ | Intelligent life reaches a new planet |
| 2️⃣ | It brings life with it — crops, animals, microbes, viruses |
| 3️⃣ | That life starts evolving on its own, under the new world's rules |
| 4️⃣ | Colonies grow, and reach out again |

> 💡 **Tip:** More planets alive = more [Souls](souls-and-harvesting.md "The energy you spend on god powers") and a bigger score.

## 🌞 Dyson spheres

A [Dyson sphere](glossary.md#dyson-sphere "A shell of machines built around a star to catch all its energy") is a giant shell of machines built around a star to catch its energy. It's the mark of a truly advanced civilization.

## 🌌 The galactic mind

Spread far enough and a species can grow a **[galactic mind](glossary.md#galactic-mind "One will steering life across a whole galaxy")**, the top layer of [how life thinks](groups-and-flocking.md#-how-life-thinks "Layered mini AIs"). Steps 71–100 of the [grouping ladder](groups-and-flocking.md "The 100 steps") run from the first off-world colony to universe-spanning life.

## ⚔️ Intergalactic war

Galaxies are big enough for more than one empire. Civilizations meet. They trade. They fight.

| | |
|:-:|---|
| 🤝 | Trade and alliances |
| ⚔️ | War between worlds — and between galaxies |
| 💀 | Self-destruction: intelligent life has amazing ways of killing itself |

> 😈 *Every war makes souls. You don't have to stop them.*
