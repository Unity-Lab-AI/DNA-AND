## ☄️ Impacts

Comets and meteorites really hit planets. Some fall in from the **Oort cloud** or the **Kuiper belt**, some from **asteroid belts**, and some are sent by you (Meteor, Comet, Genebank Meteorite). Bigger rocks hit harder:

| # | Size | Rock | What happens | Lives lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dust** | Less than 1 m | Burns up as a shooting star | None |
| 2 | **Pebble** | ~1 m | A fireball, a few stones reach the ground | None |
| 3 | **Boulder** | ~10 m | An airburst that breaks windows | Small, local |
| 4 | **House-size** | ~20 m | A blast over a city | Hundreds |
| 5 | **Stadium-size** | ~50 m | Flattens a forest | A region |
| 6 | **City-killer** | ~150 m | Wipes out a city | A city's worth |
| 7 | **Country-killer** | ~1 km | Firestorms and winter across a continent | Millions |
| 8 | **Extinction** | ~10 km | Global firestorm, years of dark, mass extinction | Most of life |
| 9 | **World-breaker** | ~100 km | Boils the oceans, melts the crust | Nearly everything |
| 10 | **Planet collision** | Planet-size | Two worlds merge or shatter; a new moon may form | Everything |

> 🛡️ Civilizations with telescopes can see impacts coming. With rockets, they can try to push the rock aside. Without them, they can only pray.
