## 🦠 What makes something alive

Every living thing on your world does all of these. Viruses do only some of them, which is why they need other life to copy themselves.

| | It… | What that means in the game |
|:-:|---|---|
| 🧫 | **Is made of cells** | One cell or trillions. Viruses use someone else's |
| 🔥 | **Uses energy** | From light, from chemicals at hot vents, from eating others, from fermenting or breathing oxygen. Moving, growing and breeding all cost energy |
| 🌡️ | **Keeps itself steady** | Each species stays within its own limits of heat, water, salt and acid. Push it outside them and it weakens and dies |
| 🌱 | **Grows** | From small to adult, its body built step by step from its genes |
| 👁️ | **Senses and reacts** | Toward food and light, away from poison and danger. Later: eyes, ears, smell, electric senses |
| 🥚 | **Breeds** | Splitting, budding, spores, seeds, eggs or live birth, with or without sex |
| 🧬 | **Passes on its genes** | Young carry their parents' genes, with small mistakes (mutations) |
| 🔁 | **Evolves** | Over generations, the ones that fit best leave the most young. One creature never changes its own genes to fit |

## 🍽️ Who eats whom

Every region of your world has its own food web.

| | Level | Lives on |
|:-:|---|---|
| ☀️ | **Producers** | Light (or vent chemicals) and the nutrients in the water and soil |
| 🐌 | **Grazers** | Producers |
| 🦈 | **Hunters** | Other animals |
| 🦅 | **Top hunters** | Hunters |
| 🦴 | **Scavengers** | The dead |
| 🍄 | **Decomposers** | Whatever is left. Fungi and bacteria rot it back into nutrients |

> 💡 **Energy is lost at every step.** That's why there are always far fewer hunters than prey, and far more plants than anything else.

## 🤝 Six ways species get along (or don't)

| | Kind | Who wins | Example |
|:-:|---|---|---|
| 🦁 | **Predation** (and grazing) | One gains, one loses | A wolf and a deer. A cow and grass |
| ⚔️ | **Competition** | Both lose | Two kinds of tree racing for the same light |
| 💐 | **Mutualism** | Both gain | Bees and flowers. Root fungi and plants. Gut bacteria |
| 🐟 | **Commensalism** | One gains, the other doesn't notice | A remora riding a shark |
| 🪱 | **Parasitism** | One gains, one loses, slowly | A tapeworm living inside its host |
| 🧪 | **Amensalism** | One is harmed, the other gets nothing | A mould's poison killing the bacteria around it |

Relationships can change as genes change. A partner can turn into a parasite, and back.

## 🌍 How a living world works

| | Idea | What happens |
|:-:|---|---|
| 🔄 | **Nutrient cycles** | Carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus and water move through air, sea, rock and life, again and again |
| 📈 | **Only so much room** | Each place can hold only so much life. Food comes, numbers boom; food goes, they crash |
| 🧩 | **Niches** | Each species has its own way of living. Two can't share exactly the same one for long |
| 🗝️ | **Keystone species** | Some species hold a whole system together. Lose one, and the rest can collapse |
| 🚢 | **Invaders** | Life carried to a new place can take over where nothing eats it |
| 🌾 | **Succession** | Bare rock, then lichen and moss, grass, bushes, forest. After a fire or flood, it starts again |
| 🏜️ | **Biomes** | Heat, water and light make deserts, grasslands, forests, tundra, reefs and the deep sea |
| 🍂 | **Seasons, day and night** | Life times its breeding, sleeping, hibernating and migrating to them |

## 🧬 How new species appear

| | Force | What it does |
|:-:|---|---|
| 🏆 | **Natural selection** | The best fit leave the most young |
| 🦚 | **Choosing mates** | Displays, songs, horns and colours evolve because mates pick them |
| 🎲 | **Chance** | In small groups, genes change just by luck |
| 🔀 | **Mixing** | Groups that meet mix their genes |
| 🏔️ | **Splitting** | Groups cut off by seas, mountains or distance drift apart into new species |
| 🧬 | **Crossing** | Close species can cross, and now and then a new species comes of it |
| 🦇 | **The same answer twice** | Wings and eyes evolve again and again in unrelated life |
| 💀 | **Die-offs and comebacks** | After a mass extinction, survivors spread fast into the emptied world |

## 🪜 The great steps of life

Life on your world has to climb these on its own. You can only nudge.

1. 🫧 Self-copying molecules gather inside tiny bubbles
2. 🧵 Genes join into chromosomes
3. 🧬 DNA and proteins take over from RNA
4. 🔬 Simple cells become complex cells with a nucleus, by swallowing smaller cells that become their power plants
5. 💞 Sex: two parents mix their genes
6. 🪸 Many-celled life, with cells doing different jobs
7. 🐜 Colonies where most members never breed
8. 🗣️ Societies held together by language

See [Stages of Life](stages-of-life.md "All 100 stages, from chemistry to the stars") for every step along the way.

## 🌋 Life changes its world

- 🫁 **Air:** life fills the air with oxygen, which builds the ozone shield. Some microbes fill it with methane and warm the world
- 🪨 **Rock and soil:** reefs, chalk and coal are made by life. Soil comes from roots and rot
- 🌏 **And the world changes life:** drifting continents, ice ages, warming, impacts and volcanoes reshape who lives where
