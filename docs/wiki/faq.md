# ❓ FAQ

[🏠 Wiki home](README.md) · [📖 Glossary](glossary.md)

**100 questions**, in 10 groups.

---

## 🎮 The game

**1. Is this a clicker game?**  
**No.** Life doesn't grow because you tap a button. It evolves on its own, from real-style DNA. You're the god who nudges it.

**2. Who is it for?**  
Adults. **DNA-AND is an 18+ game.** Life does everything real life does: mating, hunting, killing, war, conquest and murder.

**3. What kind of game is it?**  
A god game and life simulation. Think Populous, with real genes inside every living thing.

**4. Do I control the creatures?**  
No. You nudge them with [god powers](god-powers.md). They decide what to do.

**5. Is there a story?**  
Your galaxy writes it. Every world plays out differently.

**6. Can I win?**  
It's open-ended. You play until you start a new world and galaxy.

**7. Can I lose?**  
Not by force. Even if all life dies, the game stays open until you restart.

**8. How long is a game?**  
About **4–6 hours** from the first cell to a space-faring civilization. After that, it's open-ended. See [Time](time.md).

**9. Is it a simulation or a game?**  
Both. It runs like a simulation, and you play it with god mode.

**10. Is it made with an engine?**  
No. Everything is built from scratch, even the tools that build it.

## 🌍 Worlds

**11. Is it only Earth?**  
Earth comes first. Other [planet templates](planets-and-templates.md) unlock as you play, and your galaxy is full of solar systems.

**12. How many planet templates are there?**  
100. The first after Earth are Ocean, Desert, Ice, Jungle, Volcanic, Tidally locked and Gas giant moon.

**13. How big is the universe?**  
Galaxy-sized, with stars, planets, solar systems and more than one galaxy.

**14. Can I pick the galaxy size?**  
Yes: Small (100 star systems), Medium (500), Large (2,000) or Massive (10,000). Every system is fully simulated.

**15. Can I replay the same world?**  
Yes. Use the same [seed](glossary.md#seed "The number that builds your world — same seed, same galaxy").

**16. Does the planet change on its own?**  
Yes. Plates move, weather turns, oceans rise and fall.

**17. Are all 118 elements in?**  
Yes. Lab-made ones (93 and up) unlock once a civilization creates them. See [Elements](elements.md).

**18. Can life reach other planets by itself?**  
No. Intelligent life has to carry it there, by ship or by your Life Seed power.

**19. Do stars die?**  
Yes. Stars can be brightened, dimmed or blown up by your powers.

**20. Is there weather?**  
Rain, storms, snow, wind, seasons, day and night.

## 🧬 Life and genes

**21. How does life start?**  
With your first action. Life never starts without you.

**22. Is the DNA real?**  
Real-style: A, C, G and T, building every body and behavior. See [Genes & DNA](genes-and-dna.md).

**23. Can I edit a creature's DNA?**  
Not directly. You **nudge** with powers: add genes, add elements, raise mutation, push evolution toward a trait. Gene powers can go well or badly.

**24. Do viruses evolve too?**  
Yes, and they carry genes between species.

**25. How do new species appear?**  
When a group's genes drift far enough apart, it splits in two.

**26. Can a species go extinct?**  
Yes, and it stays greyed out on the [Tree of Life](tree-of-life.md) forever.

**27. How many stages of life are there?**  
100, from chemistry to spacefarers. See [Stages of Life](stages-of-life.md).

**28. Why do creatures fight so much?**  
Every gene is selfish. All life tries to get more and do more before death.

**29. Do creatures die of old age?**  
Yes. Death always comes.

**30. Can life breed without sex?**  
Yes. Some life copies itself, and some needs two parents.

## 🐺 Groups and minds

**31. Can animals other than apes become intelligent?**  
Yes. **All things flock together.** Wolves, bees, birds, fish, even plants can climb the 100-step [grouping ladder](groups-and-flocking.md "Lone creature to universe-spanning life") all the way to civilization.

**32. What is the grouping ladder?**  
100 steps from one lone creature to universe-spanning life: pack, horde, tribe, village, city, nation, and on to the stars.

**33. Do brainless things act?**  
Yes, through chemistry. Bacteria swarm toward food and "vote" before they attack.

**34. What are the mini AIs?**  
Every living thing runs one, in layers: chemistry, individual, group, hive, species, galactic mind.

**35. Can a hive mind form?**  
Yes, if evolution builds the bond for it.

**36. Can different species team up?**  
Yes: they help, tame, farm, rule, enslave and merge. There are 100 ways species mix.

**37. Can a species rebel against its group?**  
Yes. Bigger minds steer smaller ones, but selfish genes cheat and split off.

**38. Do I see every single worm?**  
Not always. One worm on screen can stand for 4,396 worms. Zoom in to see individuals.

**39. What's the Tree of Life?**  
A circular family tree and gene tree of every species, sortable by planet, solar system or galaxy.

**40. How deep does the Tree of Life go?**  
Every rank, from domain to species, is tracked 100 deep.

## 🏛️ Civilizations

**41. How many ages are there?**  
100, from first tools to intergalactic. See [Ages of Civilization](ages-of-civilization.md).

**42. Do ages always go in the same order?**  
On Earth they follow real history. Other templates can take their own order.

**43. Can a civilization go backward?**  
Yes. A collapse can throw them back ages.

**44. How do civilizations destroy themselves?**  
In 100 ways, from nuclear winter to rogue machines.

**45. What kinds of society are there?**  
100 kinds of rule, trade, family, belief and law. See [Society](society.md).

**46. Do I pick their government?**  
No. You [nudge](nudges.md) with omens, prophets, peace and discord.

**47. How many tech branches are there?**  
100, in 10 families. See [Tech Tree](tech-tree.md).

**48. Do civilizations know about me?**  
They can notice you, especially if they see you harvesting.

**49. Can they worship me?**  
Yes. Omens and miracles shape what they believe.

**50. Can they fight each other?**  
Yes: wars, conquests, slavery and genocide.

## ⚡ Powers

**51. How many powers are there?**  
100. Each has a use, a plus and a minus. See [God Powers](god-powers.md).

**52. What do I start with?**  
Fire, Cold, Water, Lightning, Spark of Life, adding genes and adding elements.

**53. How do powers unlock?**  
Each of the 100 stages of life unlocks one power.

**54. What are tiers?**  
T1 to T100: every power ranked weakest to strongest. Higher tiers cost more.

**55. What does a power cost?**  
Tier × area × strength, paid in souls.

**56. Do powers have cooldowns?**  
Only the 20 strongest (T81–T100).

**57. What's the strongest power?**  
Supernova (T100).

**58. Can powers backfire?**  
Yes. Every one has a minus.

**59. Can I protect an area?**  
Yes, with Sanctuary.

**60. Can I see disasters coming?**  
Yes, with Forecast.

## 👻 Souls

**61. What are Souls?**  
Your currency. Life gives them off, and deaths and [harvesting](souls-and-harvesting.md) give more. Every power costs souls.

**62. Where do souls come from?**  
100 sources: life, death, harvesting, disasters, and civilizations harvesting for you.

**63. Which deaths pay the most?**  
Intelligent ones.

**64. What is harvesting?**  
Reaping souls by hand. It's slow, it kills what you reap, and it glows.

**65. What happens if they see me harvest?**  
Intelligent life panics.

**66. Do milestones give souls?**  
No.

**67. Can civilizations harvest for me?**  
Yes. Every one of the 100 ages adds a new way they do it.

**68. Is a volcano on a city worth it?**  
It pays a lot of souls. Whether it's worth it is up to you.

**69. Does harvesting hurt my score?**  
It lowers life in your galaxy, but life harvested counts too.

**70. What's the endgame harvest?**  
Intelligent life spreads across galaxies, using everything up.

## ⏱️ Time and controls

**71. Can I pause?**  
Yes, with Space.

**72. How many speeds are there?**  
Pause plus 1×, 10×, 100×, 1,000× and max.

**73. Why does time slow down by itself?**  
At each stage's headline milestone, time drops to 1× and a short cinematic plays. The other 99 milestones in a stage just show a notice.

**74. Can I turn the cinematics off?**  
Yes, in Settings → Gameplay.

**75. How do I zoom?**  
Scroll wheel, from one microbe to a whole galaxy.

**76. How do I move the camera?**  
WASD or the screen edges, and right-drag to rotate.

**77. How do I use a power?**  
Pick it and left-click.

**78. Can I rebind keys?**  
Yes, every key.

**79. How do I open the wiki in game?**  
Press F1.

**80. Is there accessibility support?**  
Yes: colorblind modes, text size, subtitles and reduced motion.

## 👥 Multiplayer and online

**81. Can I play with friends?**  
Yes: co-op and multiplayer, with one player hosting and everyone's cursors on screen. See [Multiplayer](multiplayer.md).

**82. How many players?**  
Up to 8 per world.

**83. How do friends join?**  
The host shares a join code.

**84. Do we share one world?**  
Yes, all working toward one goal.

**85. Do I need the internet?**  
Only for the leaderboard, the Genebank and online multiplayer.

**86. How is my leaderboard score worked out?**  
**Life in your galaxy + life harvested.** See [Leaderboard & Genebank](leaderboard-and-genebank.md).

**87. What is the Genebank?**  
A shared library of players' genomes.

**88. Who can upload to the Genebank?**  
The weekly top three: Genome of the Week.

**89. How do I use someone else's genome?**  
Pick it from the Genebank. It arrives in your world by meteorite.

**90. Can people cheat?**  
The creator's answer: "no cheats will NOT work its fine."

## 🛠️ Install and tech

**91. What can I play it on?**  
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Mac and Linux are planned for later.

**92. Where do I download it?**  
From the release pages on [Unity AI Lab Git](https://git.unityailab.com/GFourteen/DNA-AND/releases) or [GitHub](https://github.com/Unity-Lab-AI/DNA-AND/releases).

**93. What PC do I need?**  
Minimum 4 cores, 8 GB RAM and a Direct3D 12 card with 4 GB. See [Setup](../SETUP.md).

**94. How does it save?**  
Autosave every 10 minutes and at every headline milestone, plus unlimited save slots.

**95. Does updating delete my saves?**  
No. A newer setup updates the game and keeps your saves.

**96. Does uninstalling delete my saves?**  
It asks you first.

**97. Does it run in a browser?**  
No. It's its own program in its own window.

**98. Why build everything from scratch?**  
The project's first rule: no one else's code, scripts, programs or compilers.

**99. What language is it written in?**  
Helix, our own language, built from raw machine bytes up.

**100. Who made it?**  
The Unity AI Lab team. See [Credits](credits.md).
