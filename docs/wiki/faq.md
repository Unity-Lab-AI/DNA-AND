# ❓ FAQ

[🏠 Wiki home](README.md)

---

### 🎮 Is this a clicker game?
**No.** Life doesn't grow because you tap a button. It evolves on its own, from real-style DNA. You're the god who nudges it.

### 🔞 Who is it for?

Adults. **DNA-AND is an 18+ game.** Life does everything real life does: mating, hunting, killing, war, conquest and murder.

### 🐺 Can animals other than apes become intelligent?

Yes. **All things flock together.** Wolves, bees, birds, fish, even plants can climb the 100-step [grouping ladder](groups-and-flocking.md "Lone creature to universe-spanning life") all the way to civilization.

### 🧬 Can I edit a creature's DNA?
Not directly. You **nudge** with powers: add genes, add elements, raise mutation, push evolution toward a trait. Gene powers can go well or badly. See [Genes & DNA](genes-and-dna.md).

### ⌛ How long is a game?
About **4–6 hours** from the first cell to a space-faring civilization. After that, it's open-ended. See [Time](time.md).

### 💀 What happens if all life dies?
Nothing forces you out. The game stays open-ended until you restart with a new world and galaxy.

### 👻 What are Souls?
Your currency. Life gives them off, and deaths and [harvesting](souls-and-harvesting.md) give more. Every power costs souls.

### 🏆 How is my leaderboard score worked out?
**Life in your galaxy + life harvested.** See [Leaderboard & Genebank](leaderboard-and-genebank.md).

### ☄️ How do I use someone else's genome?
Pick it from the Genebank. It arrives in your world by meteorite.

### 🤝 Can I play with friends?
Yes: co-op and multiplayer, with one player hosting and everyone's cursors on screen. See [Multiplayer](multiplayer.md).

### 🌐 Do I need the internet?
Only for the leaderboard, the Genebank and online multiplayer.

### 🖥️ What can I play it on?
Windows. Mac and Linux are planned for later.

### 🪐 Is it only Earth?
Earth comes first. Other planet [templates](planets-and-templates.md) unlock as you play, and your galaxy is full of solar systems.

### 🎲 Can I replay the same world?
Yes. Use the same [seed](glossary.md#seed "The number that builds your world — same seed, same galaxy").
