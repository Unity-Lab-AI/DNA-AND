## 1 · 🏠 Main menu and a new world

<!-- IMG: docs/img/main-menu.png | The DNA-AND main menu over a slowly turning galaxy -->
> 🖼️ *The DNA-AND main menu over a slowly turning galaxy* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

| Menu | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🌍 **New World** | Random seed or your own, then pick a galaxy size |
| 📂 **Continue / Load** | Your save slots |
| 👥 **Multiplayer** | Host or join. The host's computer runs the world |
| 🧬 **Genebank + Leaderboard** | Top players and their uploaded genomes |
| 📖 **Wiki** | Everything in the game, explained |

Pick **New World**, leave the seed random, and press start. The galaxy builds itself: [stars, planets, solar systems and galaxies](planets-and-templates.md "Stars and planets make solar systems, which make galaxies"). The camera dives toward one rocky world. Your first planet uses the **Earth** template.

## 2 · 🌑 The dead planet

Bare rock. Boiling seas. Poison air. Nothing alive.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/dead-planet.png | A top-down view of a barren volcanic world, steam rising from shallow seas -->
> 🖼️ *A top-down view of a barren volcanic world, steam rising from shallow seas* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

**Your job:** make the world fit for life. You have the [starting toolkit](god-powers.md#-your-starting-toolkit "Lightning, heat, cold, water, spark of life, add genes, add elements"):

1. ❄️ **Cold** the boiling seas down.
2. 💧 **Water** to fill the low basins into oceans.
3. ⚗️ **Add Elements:** carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus. All 118 are on the [Elements](elements.md "The full periodic table, and what each element does") page.
4. ⚡ **Lightning** into the shallows to stir the chemistry.

> 💡 **Tip:** use [Pause](time.md "Pause, 1x, 10x, 100x, 1,000x and max") ⏸️ often. Shaping a planet goes better slowly.

> ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** too much heat and the oceans boil off. Too much cold and the whole world freezes over. Neither is the end, but each costs you time.

---

## 3 · ✨ The spark

Conditions look right: warm shallow water full of chemistry. Cast ✨ **Spark of Life**.

> "life starts base on your first action"

The first cells flicker in a tide pool. The game **slows down automatically** so you can watch this milestone happen.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/first-cells.png | Close zoom into a tide pool, the first glowing single cells drifting in the water -->
> 🖼️ *Close zoom into a tide pool, the first glowing single cells drifting in the water* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

From here on, souls start trickling in. 👻 [Souls](glossary.md#souls "The energy you spend on god powers") come from living things, so the more life there is, the faster they come.

---

## 4 · 🦠 The age of microbes

Scroll all the way in. Every microbe has a real-style [genome](genes-and-dna.md "Real-style DNA: the letters that build every living thing"). Scroll out, and they show up as glowing mats and clouds, grouped by where they live.

- 🦠 **Viruses** appear and start swapping genes around.
- 🌿 Some cells learn **photosynthesis**. The air slowly fills with oxygen.
- 🍄 Decomposers, partnerships, parasites: the first food chains form.

> 💡 **Tip:** 🫧 **More Oxygen** and 🛡️ **Ozone** help life prepare for land later.

> ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** a virus wipes out your best microbe line. That's evolution. Something new grows into the gap.

Follow your progress through the [stages of life](stages-of-life.md "100 stages, each with its own milestones").

---

## 5 · 🐟 Life in the seas

Cells clump into many-celled life. Then come jellies, worms, shells, the first fish.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/early-seas.png | A shallow reef of early sea creatures under rippling light -->
> 🖼️ *A shallow reef of early sea creatures under rippling light* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

- 🎯 **Pressure** nudges life toward a trait, like a backbone. No guarantees.
- 🌊 **Ocean Current** carries warmth and food to new waters.
- 🔭 **Watcher** keeps track of one species across the planet.

> ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** a 💀 **Plague** spreads through a crowded reef. One worm can stand for thousands, and so can its disease.

### 🧬 Genebank moment

Another player's uploaded genome arrives. A **Genebank Meteorite** ☄️ splashes into your ocean and a new lineage joins the race. It might thrive. It might take over. See [Leaderboard & Genebank](leaderboard-and-genebank.md "Top players, their uploaded genomes, and how meteorites bring them to you").

---

## 6 · 🌿 Life takes the land

Plants creep onto the shore. Insects follow. Fish grow legs and breathe air. Each step is a milestone, and the game slows down so you can watch it.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/first-land.png | The first green plants creeping across a rocky shoreline, an early insect on a leaf -->
> 🖼️ *The first green plants creeping across a rocky shoreline, an early insect on a leaf* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

| Milestone | Watch for |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Plants on land | Green edges along coasts |
| 🐜 Insects | Swarms, pollination |
| 🦎 Walking | The first steps out of the water |
| 🪶 Flight | Wings, in insects first, then others |

> 💡 **Tip:** 🏞️ **Rivers** and 🌱 **Fertile Soil** turn bare continents green fast.

> ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** a 🌋 volcano winter or a ☄️ **Meteor** can wipe out the big animals. Mass extinctions clear the stage for whatever survives.

---

## 7 · 🧠 A mind awakens

One species starts using tools. In this run it's an ape, but it could be wolves, crows or octopuses ([all things flock together](groups-and-flocking.md "Any line of life can climb to intelligence")). Cast 🧠 **Spark of Mind**, the biggest gamble in the game.

> 🧠 **Watch the minds stack up:** the tribe gets a group AI, then the whole species one. Every member still wants its own genes to win, so expect betrayal. See [How life thinks](groups-and-flocking.md#-how-life-thinks "Layered mini AIs, from chemistry to galactic mind").

> "intelligent life has amazing ways of killing its self"

From here on you **nudge** intelligent life. You never command it. You have the powers people credit God with.

- 🌠 **Omens** and 🙌 **Miracles** shape what tribes believe.
- 🧙 **Prophets** unite tribes, or become tyrants.

> ⚠️ **Harvesting gets risky.** Reaping souls by hand is slow, and if intelligent life **sees** you do it, they panic. See [Souls & Harvesting](souls-and-harvesting.md "Where souls come from and the price of taking them").

---

## 8 · 🏛️ Civilization rises

Fire, language, tribes, farming, metal, empires. The [Ages of Civilization](ages-of-civilization.md "100 ages of real history, from stone tools to intergalactic empires") and the [Tech Tree](tech-tree.md "Every technology civilizations can discover") take over.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/first-cities.png | A river valley at dusk dotted with early farms and the lights of a first city -->
> 🖼️ *A river valley at dusk dotted with early farms and the lights of a first city* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

| Nudge | Use it when |
|---|---|
| 🌾 **Harvest** | A young people is starving |
| 💡 **Inspiration** | You want a discovery sooner |
| 🦉 **Wisdom** | A nation is heading for self-destruction |
| ⚔️ **Discord** | One empire is getting too dangerous |

> 🌋 **The dark trade.** A volcano under a city makes **lots of souls**. Your [score](leaderboard-and-genebank.md "Score = life in your galaxy + life harvested") counts life harvested, but it also counts life alive. Choose carefully.

> ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** world wars, plagues spread by trade, poisoned air, weapons that end everything. Every civilization can die.

### 👥 Multiplayer moment

In co-op, you **see each other's cursors** on the same world. One of you calms a war with 🕊️ **Peace** while the other hands out a good 🌾 **Harvest**. Everyone works toward one goal. See [Multiplayer](multiplayer.md "Single, co-op and multiplayer in one shared world").

Later, intelligent life starts **harvesting for you**, using its own power to reap life on your behalf.

---

## 9 · 🚀 The space age (≈ 4–6 hours in)

The first rocket leaves the planet. **Milestone.** The game slows to let you watch.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/first-launch.png | A rocket climbing from a coastal city on a column of fire, the planet's curve below -->
> 🖼️ *A rocket climbing from a coastal city on a column of fire, the planet's curve below* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

Space powers open up: 🪐 **Orbit Nudge**, 🔆 **Star Brighten**, 🌀 **Wormhole**.

> ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** a 🌞 **Solar Flare** fries satellites and tech. Your own power can knock a young space age back down.

---

## 10 · 🪐 Other worlds

> "to get to other planbets intelligent life has to get there first"

Your civilization lands on a new planet, and life can spread there. New [planet templates](planets-and-templates.md "Earth first, then other templates you unlock") unlock, with other worlds that evolve in other orders.

- 🌱☄️ **Life Seed** sends life ahead by rock.
- Colonies grow. Each world has its own evolution.

---

## 11 · ☀️ The Dyson sphere

A civilization wraps its star in a shell of collectors. It's the biggest structure life can build. See [Space & Dyson Spheres](space-and-dyson-spheres.md "Space travel, colonies, Dyson spheres, war across galaxies").

<!-- IMG: docs/img/dyson-sphere.png | A star half-enclosed by a lattice of glowing Dyson-sphere panels -->
> 🖼️ *A star half-enclosed by a lattice of glowing Dyson-sphere panels* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

> "since all life is like a virus advanced forms seprad and use up avasil nutrientrs and resources and have to spread"

Advanced life has used up what it has. It has to spread again.

---

## 12 · ⚔️ War among galaxies

Other civilizations, maybe your own split-off colonies, meet across the void. Intergalactic war begins.

> ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** everything. ⚫ **Black Holes**, 💥 **Supernovas** and weapons that empty whole systems. All life can die.

---

## 13 · ♾️ The open end

There's no final screen. The galaxy goes on: life rises, falls and rises again.

> "open ended till u resarts to get a new world and galaxy"

When you're ready, go back to the main menu and start a **new world and galaxy**. Your score goes to the [Leaderboard](leaderboard-and-genebank.md "Top players and the Genebank"):

| Score part | |
|---|---|
| 🌍 Life in your galaxy | Everything alive |
| 👻 Life harvested | Every soul you took |

Finish in the weekly top three and you can upload a genome to the **Genebank**, ready to land in someone else's ocean.
