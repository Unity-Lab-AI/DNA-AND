## 🐢 Milestone slow-downs

When something big happens — the first walk, the first fire, the first farm, the first rocket — the game **slows itself down** so you can see it.

> 🎬 *You don't miss the moment a fish crawls onto land.*

Then you speed back up. Each [milestone](glossary.md#milestone "A big step in evolution or history, like first walking or first fire") you pass moves the clock into a new rhythm.

👉 All 100 stages and their milestones are in [Stages of Life](stages-of-life.md).

## ⌛ How long is a game?

| Stretch | Real-world time |
|---|---|
| 🦠 → 🚀 First cell to a space-faring civilization | About **4–6 hours** |
| 🚀 → 🌌 Space age to galactic empires | Open-ended |

The early ages fly by in billions of years. History slows down to centuries, then decades. Everything balances so your evening reaches the stars. ✨

---

## ♾️ Does it ever end?

No. Your galaxy keeps going — until **you** restart and roll a new world and a new galaxy.

> 💀 Even if all life dies, the game doesn't force you out. Start again on the same rock, or roll a new one.
