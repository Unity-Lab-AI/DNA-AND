## 🌍 Earth — your first world

Every game starts on an Earth-like planet with **everything** a real Earth has:

| | |
|:-:|---|
| 🏔️ | Plates, mountains, valleys, canyons, volcanoes |
| 🌊 | Oceans, ocean zones, currents, tides, rivers, lakes, ice |
| 🌦️ | Air, weather, climate, seasons, day and night |
| 🪨 | Rocks, soils, minerals and all 118 [elements](elements.md) |
| 🌳 | Every environment, from deep sea vents to deserts to rainforests |

It's procedurally generated from your [seed](glossary.md#seed "The number that builds your world — same seed, same galaxy"), so no two Earths are the same.

## 🔓 Other templates

Reach certain points on Earth and you unlock new planet [templates](glossary.md#template "A planet type with its own rules, unlocked by reaching milestones") — new kinds of worlds with their own rules.

> 🧬 On Earth, life follows the [100 stages of life](stages-of-life.md) in order. **On other templates, life can evolve in other orders.**

👉 The full list of planet templates is one of the top-100 lists (category 62).

🔓 **The first templates to unlock:** Ocean world, Desert world, Ice world, Jungle world, Volcanic world, Tidally locked world and Gas giant moon. There are 100 in all.

## 🌌 Where planets live

```
🪐 Planets + ⭐ stars  →  ☀️ Solar systems  →  🌌 Galaxies
```

Your galaxy is full of solar systems. Each one has its own star and planets — and each planet is a place life could one day reach.
