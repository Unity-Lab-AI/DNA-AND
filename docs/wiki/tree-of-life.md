# 🌳 Tree of Life

> *Every creature that ever lived in your galaxy, on one living, branching circle.*

<!-- IMG: docs/img/tree-of-life-circle.png | A glowing circular tree of life: the first cell at the center, branches fanning out to thousands of species around the rim, extinct branches greyed out -->
> 🖼️ *A glowing circular tree of life, the first cell at the center and thousands of species around the rim* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

The Tree of Life tracks **every species, every ancestor and every gene line** in your game. It grows by itself as life evolves.

---

## 🌀 The circular tree

| | |
|--|--|
| 🎯 **Center** | Your first living cell, the ancestor of everything |
| 🌿 **Branches** | Each split is a new species being born |
| 🔘 **Rim** | Every species alive right now |
| 🩶 **Grey branches** | Extinct lines. They're kept forever |
| 🔍 **Zoom** | Spin and zoom from the whole tree down to one family |

Click any branch to see that species' [genome](glossary.md#genome "The full set of DNA instructions for one living thing"), parents, children and gene map.

---

## 🧬 The two trees

| Tree | What it shows |
|---|---|
| 👪 **Family tree** (heredity) | Who descended from whom: parents, children, ancestors |
| 🧬 **Gene tree** | Where each **gene** came from and how it spread, including genes that viruses carried between species |

---

## 🪜 Every rank, 100 deep

Living things are sorted into ranks, like real biology. **Every rank tracks the top 100**: real ones, plus new ones your world invents as it evolves.

| Rank | Example |
|---|---|
| 🌐 Domain | Bacteria, Archaea, Eukaryotes… |
| 👑 Kingdom | Animals, Plants, Fungi… |
| 🧱 Phylum | Animals with backbones, insects and spiders… |
| 🏷️ Class | Mammals, birds, fish… |
| 📂 Order | Meat-eaters, rodents… |
| 🏠 Family | Dogs and wolves, cats… |
| 🔖 Genus | Wolves and jackals… |
| 🐺 Species | Grey wolf… |

> 🧪 **Real plus invented:** real biology has only a few domains and kingdoms. Your world can evolve brand new ones, so every rank fills up to 100.

---

## 🔃 Sort it any way

| Sort by | Shows |
|---|---|
| 🌍 **Planet** | Life on one world |
| ☀️ **Solar system** | Life across every world around one star |
| 🌌 **Galaxy** | Everything, everywhere |

Also filter by alive or extinct, by [stage of life](stages-of-life.md "100 stages from chemistry to spacefaring"), or by [group step](groups-and-flocking.md "The 100-step grouping ladder").
