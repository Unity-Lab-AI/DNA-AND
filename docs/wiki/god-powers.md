# ⚡ God Powers

> *You don't build life. You make the world it has to survive in.*

<!-- IMG: docs/img/powers-banner.png | A god's-eye view of a young planet: lightning striking a steaming ocean, a volcano glowing on the coast -->
> 🖼️ *A god's-eye view of a young planet: lightning striking a steaming ocean, a volcano glowing on the coast* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

DNA-AND has **100 god powers**. Every one has a use, a plus and a minus. None of them is "just good." Fire warms a frozen world, and it also burns what lives there.

---

## 📜 The one rule: you never touch life directly

You can't edit a creature. You can't place a species, pick its traits or tell a tribe what to do.

> "no direct edit besids using powers to influence things to happen"

You change the **world**, and life answers. Raise a mountain and the herds split. Flood a valley and the fish move in. Life decides what to do next, through its genes and through [evolution](genes-and-dna.md "Real-style DNA that mutates, mixes and gets passed on").

Even the gene powers work this way. They push evolution and raise the odds. They never write the answer.

---

## 👻 Paying for powers: Souls

Every power costs [Souls](glossary.md#souls "The energy you spend on god powers"). Full details are on [Souls & Harvesting](souls-and-harvesting.md).

| Where souls come from | How it feels |
|---|---|
| 🌱 **Living things** slowly give off souls | The more life in your galaxy, the faster they trickle in |
| 🫳 **Harvesting** life by hand | Works, but it's **slow**, and it **freaks out intelligent life** if they see it |
| 🌋 **Disasters** that kill many at once | A volcano going off under a city makes **lots of souls** |
| 🤖 **Intelligent life harvesting for you** (later) | They use their own powers to reap life on your behalf |

> ⚠️ **The trade-off.** Souls come from death, but your score comes from life. Every soul you take costs your galaxy something.

On a dead planet, on day one, you have:

| | Power | Why you need it |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ | **Lightning** | Sparks chemistry in the early oceans |
| 🔥 | **Heat** | Warms a frozen world |
| ❄️ | **Cold** | Cools a boiling one |
| 💧 | **Water** | Fills basins, makes seas |
| ✨ | **Spark of Life** | Tries to start the first cells |
| 🧬 | **Add Genes** | Puts genetic material into the world (life still decides what to do with it) |
| ⚗️ | **Add Elements** | Adds any of the **118 elements** of the periodic table. See [Elements](elements.md "All 118 elements, and what life and civilization use them for") |

> 💡 **Tip:** life only starts when the conditions are right. Use the toolkit to set the stage first, then try the spark.
