## 🎮 Three ways to play

| | Mode | What it is |
|:-:|---|---|
| 👤 | **Single player** | You and your galaxy |
| 👥 | **Co-op** | Friends working together on one galaxy |
| 🌐 | **Multiplayer** | More players in one shared world, all working to one goal |

---

## 🏠 Hosting

One player's computer **hosts** the world. Everyone else joins it.

1. 🖥️ The [host](glossary.md#host "The player whose computer runs the shared world") picks **Multiplayer → Host** from the main menu.
2. 🔗 Friends pick **Multiplayer → Join** and connect.
3. 🌍 Everyone plays in the host's world.

🔑 The host shares a **join code**. Up to **8 players** per world.
