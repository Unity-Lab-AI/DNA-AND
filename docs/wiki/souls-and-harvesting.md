# 👻 Souls & Harvesting

> *Every living thing is a little bit of power. Every death, a little more.*

## 👻 What are Souls?

**Souls** are your currency. Every [power](god-powers.md "Your god abilities: fire, water, plague, meteor and 96 more") costs souls. No souls, no miracles.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/souls-rising.png | Faint glowing motes drifting up from a forest and a herd toward the camera -->
> 🖼️ *Souls drifting up from a living forest* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

---

## 🌿 Way 1 — Let life live

Living things slowly give off souls on their own. The more life in your world, the faster they flow.

> 💡 **Tip:** A crowded, healthy planet is a soul factory.

## 🌾 Way 2 — Harvest by hand

You can [harvest](glossary.md#harvest "Reaping living things directly for souls") an area to collect its souls directly. It works — but it has costs:

| ⚠️ Downside | What happens |
|---|---|
| 🐌 **It's slow** | Harvesting takes time |
| 😱 **It scares people** | If [intelligent life](glossary.md#intelligent-life "Species smart enough to build tools, cultures and civilizations") sees you doing it, they freak out |
| 💀 **It kills** | What you harvest is gone |

## 🌋 Way 3 — Disasters

Big deaths make big souls. A volcano going off under a city gives you **lots** of souls in one go.

> 😈 *Yes, really. You're that kind of god — if you want to be.*

---

## 🤖 Late game — Life harvests for you

Once intelligent life rises, it starts using its own powers to harvest life **for you**.

In the endgame, harvesting becomes intelligent life's own goal. Like a virus, advanced life spreads, uses up the nutrients and resources around it, and has to keep spreading.

```
🦠 Life grows → 👻 Souls flow → ⚡ You spend them → 🏙️ Civilization rises → 🌌 It harvests the stars for you
```

## 🏆 Souls and your score

Your [leaderboard](leaderboard-and-genebank.md) score is:

> **Life in your galaxy** ➕ **Life harvested**

So a thriving galaxy *and* a ruthless harvest both count.

🧠 **Intelligent deaths give the most souls.** And harvesting shows as a **visible glow**: if intelligent life nearby spots it, they get scared.
