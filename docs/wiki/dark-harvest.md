# 🩸 The Dark Harvest

[🏠 Wiki home](README.md) · [📖 Glossary](glossary.md) · [❓ FAQ](faq.md)

> 🔞 **18+.** This page covers the cruelest ways intelligent life harvests life for you.

<!-- IMG: docs/img/dark-harvest-banner.png | A burning city at night under a blood-red sky, glowing souls streaming up from it toward the god's cursor -->
> 🖼️ *A burning city under a red sky, souls streaming up toward the god's cursor* — rendered by the game's own 3D generator

Civilizations don't just harvest life by farming. As they climb the [100 ages](ages-of-civilization.md "Stone tools to intergalactic"), they find **darker ways**: madness, plague, war, cruel laws, sacrifice, slavery, poisoned worlds, gene weapons, killing machines, and culls across the stars.

Every one of these pays you [souls](souls-and-harvesting.md "The currency of a god"). You never order them. You [nudge](nudges.md "100 ways to nudge"), and they choose. And every one has a price: less life left in your galaxy, and a civilization closer to [destroying itself](ages-of-civilization.md#-how-civilizations-end "100 ways civilizations end").

| | |
|--|--|
| 📅 **When** | Each method appears at its own [age](ages-of-civilization.md) |
| ⚡ **Pushed by** | The [powers](god-powers.md) that nudge civilizations toward it |
| 👻 **Pays** | Souls from every death it causes. Intelligent deaths pay the most |
| 💀 **Costs** | Less life in your galaxy, and more risk of collapse |
