## 👻 Souls, the currency
| Source | How it works |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Living things | Slowly give off Souls. More life means more Souls |
| 🫳 Harvesting by hand | Fast, but it kills the life there. "its slow and freaks out intelligent life if seen" |
