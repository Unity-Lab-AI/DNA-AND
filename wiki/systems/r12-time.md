# ⏱️ R12 · Time

> "time and pasue all balances to run like reall time scales and can back off on the speed once certain mile stones are met" · "a full game should take about 4-6 hours to get a civ to space"

## ⏯️ Controls (decided)
| Button | Speed |
|---|---|
| ⏸️ | Pause |
| ▶️ | 1x |
| ⏩ | 10x |
| ⏩⏩ | 100x |
| ⏩⏩⏩ | 1,000x |
| ⏭️ | Max |

At each stage's **headline** milestone, the game **slows down automatically** so you can watch it happen. The other 99 milestones per stage only show a notice.

## ⚖️ Pacing
Earth took about 4 billion years. The game has to fit that into **about 4 to 6 hours to reach space**.
✅ Each stage of life has its own base "years per second". 1x means that stage's base rate, and the rates are tuned so the total lands at 4 to 6 hours. At each milestone time drops to 1x with a short cinematic, which can be turned off.
