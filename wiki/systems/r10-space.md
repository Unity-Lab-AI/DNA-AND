# 🚀 R10 · Space

> "space expansion to dysonsphere" · "to get to other planbets intelligent life has to get there first" · "ingalactic war"

## 🪜 Steps outward
| Step | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 🛰️ Orbit | Satellites. From here on, stellar flares can wreck technology ("fuck with tech") |
| 🌙 Moons and planets | First colonies, and spreading life to new worlds |
| ☀️ Dyson sphere | Using a star's full energy |
| 🌀 Galaxy | Many solar systems settled |
| 🌌 Intergalactic | War and spread between galaxies |

## 🌱 Spreading life
Once intelligent life reaches a planet, life can be planted there. The planet's template decides what can survive.

## ⚔️ Meeting other civilizations
Civilizations from different worlds can meet, trade or fight across solar systems and galaxies.

## 📌 Decided specs (build from these)

- World-condition matrix: 10 conditions × 10 levels, each needing its own tech, rated per species.
- 100-step travel ladder up to hyperdrives and intergalactic travel.
- 100 space resources and 100 kinds of colony (mining outposts, prison colonies and more), all built by the space-tech generator in the builder species' own image.
